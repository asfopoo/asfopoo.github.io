Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how EAN Solutions LLC ("EAN Solutions," "we," "us") handles data in Spacecast for Google Chat ("Spacecast"), an app for Atlassian Jira Cloud distributed through the Atlassian Marketplace. It applies to the app and to this website.

In short: Spacecast runs on Atlassian Forge. It stores only its own configuration, a 7-day delivery log, and, for rules set to an hourly or daily digest, a short summary of each update until the digest is posted, in Atlassian's hosted storage for your Jira site. It sends issue updates to the Google Chat webhooks your admins add and, for people who turn on personal notifications, as direct messages from the Spacecast app in Google Chat. We do not run our own databases for Spacecast. Personal notifications use one small sign-in service we run at auth.ean-solutions.com (hosted by Vercel), which stores nothing. We do not sell data, and we do not use your Jira content for advertising, analytics, or AI training.

1. Our role

The organization that installs Spacecast (the "customer") decides what Spacecast sends and where. For personal data in Jira content, the customer is the controller and we act as a processor (or "service provider") on its behalf. Atlassian hosts and runs the app on its Forge platform. Google receives the messages the customer chooses to send to Google Chat. Personal notifications are optional: a site admin can turn them off for the whole site, and each person decides whether to connect their own Google account.

A Chat message contains the issue key, summary, issue type, status or status change, the actor's display name, up to 600 characters of comment text, and a link back to the issue. Messages about new or reassigned issues can also include the assignee's display name, the priority, and up to 300 characters of the description. Spacecast does not read attachments.

Personal direct messages go only to the person who connected, only about issues that person can see in Jira, and never about their own changes. If a comment's visibility is restricted to a group or role, or it is an internal service desk comment, the direct message says so instead of showing the comment text.

3. Who receives data

  • Google, for space notifications. Messages are sent only to the Google Chat webhook URLs your admins configure (at chat.googleapis.com). Anyone in that Chat space can read them. Google handles them under your organization's Google Workspace agreement and Google's privacy policy. Choose the spaces and filters with that in mind.
  • Google, for personal notifications. Direct messages are sent through the Google Chat API as the Spacecast Chat app, using a Google Cloud project that EAN Solutions owns. Connecting uses Google sign-in (accounts.google.com and oauth2.googleapis.com). Google processes this data as our sub-processor (see our Data Processing Addendum) and within your organization's Google Workspace, where the messages are stored.
  • Atlassian. Atlassian hosts the app, its storage, and its runtime under the Atlassian Privacy Policy. Spacecast's network access is limited by its manifest to chat.googleapis.com and oauth2.googleapis.com.
  • Vercel, for connecting personal notifications. Our sign-in service at auth.ean-solutions.com runs on Vercel Inc. (United States). When someone connects, it receives their Google sign-in, their Google account ID and email, and their direct message space name, passes the result to Spacecast in Jira, and keeps none of it. Our own logs record only whether a sign-in succeeded; Vercel's standard request logs (IP address, time, and the request address, which contains no personal data) are kept briefly under Vercel's retention. Vercel processes this as our sub-processor (see the Data Processing Addendum).
  • No one else. We do not sell, rent, or share data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Google user data

When someone connects Google Chat, Spacecast asks Google for these permissions, and uses them only as described:

  • Your email address and basic account ID (openid, userinfo.email): to show which Google account is connected and to tie it to your Jira account.
  • View chat and spaces in Google Chat (chat.spaces.readonly): only to find your existing direct message space with the Spacecast app. Spacecast does not read messages or other spaces.
  • Create conversations and spaces in Google Chat (chat.spaces.create): only to open a direct message with the Spacecast app if you do not have one yet.

Spacecast's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, do not use it to train AI or machine learning models, and do not let people read it except with your permission, for security reasons, or where the law requires. To stop personal notifications, click Disconnect on the Spacecast: My notifications page in Jira, and remove Spacecast under Third-party apps and services in your Google account.

5. What we, EAN Solutions, can see

We cannot browse your Jira site or your app storage. Through Atlassian we receive:

  • Operational metrics and logs for the app, such as invocation counts, errors, and log lines. Our log lines contain the event type, issue key, and counts of rules and direct messages, not issue content or who received a message.
  • Licensing information from the Atlassian Marketplace, such as your site URL, license tier, and the billing and technical contacts you gave Atlassian. We use it to provide support, send service notices, and keep business and tax records.
  • Google Chat API usage for our Google Cloud project, such as request counts and errors. We cannot read your Chat messages or spaces through it.
  • Anything you send us in a support request. Please do not send webhook URLs.

The app itself sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics. This website does not use cookies or analytics either, although its host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs.

6. Security

  • Stored data stays inside Atlassian's Forge platform, with encryption in transit and at rest provided by Atlassian. The sign-in service stores nothing.
  • Webhook URLs are kept in encrypted secret storage and never returned to the browser.
  • The Spacecast Chat app's Google credentials are kept in encrypted Forge environment variables. People's Google sign-in tokens are used once by the sign-in service and then revoked; they never reach the browser or Spacecast's storage. Results from the sign-in service are signed and accepted only once, within minutes of the person clicking Connect.
  • Only Jira site admins can manage Spacecast. Project admins can manage rules for their own project only, and site admins can turn that off. Each person manages only their own personal notifications, and site admins can turn personal notifications off for everyone.
  • The app requests only the Jira scopes it needs: read Jira work, read Jira users, and app storage.

7. Retention and deletion

Delivery log entries expire after 7 days. Digest queue entries are deleted once the digest is posted, and after 8 days at most. Deleting a rule deletes its settings, webhook URL, and digest queue. Clicking Disconnect, or a site admin removing the link, deletes that person's link and preferences. When Spacecast is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the app's stored data for that site under its Forge data retention rules. Messages already posted in Google Chat stay there until deleted in Google Chat. Licensing records from the Marketplace are kept as long as needed for legal, tax, and accounting purposes.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. Because Spacecast's data lives in your organization's Jira site, please contact your Jira administrator first. We will help your organization respond to any request.
