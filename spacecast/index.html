Google Chat notifications for Jira

Spacecast sends the Jira updates your team cares about into the Google Chat spaces where it already works. A modern alternative to Google's "Google Chat for Jira Cloud" app, which hasn't been updated since 2021.

What it does

  • Rules per space. Choose projects, event types (created, moved, assigned, commented, mentioned), statuses, and an optional JQL filter.
  • People filters. Notify only when the assignee, reporter, a watcher, or a mentioned user matches.
  • One thread per issue. Updates to the same issue reply in the same Chat thread.
  • Hourly or daily digests. For busy spaces, a rule can send one summary that lists each issue once with what changed.
  • Personal notifications. Each person can get their own updates as direct messages from the Spacecast app in Google Chat. More below.
  • Project self-serve. Project admins manage their own project's rules, and site admins can turn that off.
  • Delivery log. See what was sent and anything that failed over the last 7 days.

How it works

Spacecast is built on Atlassian Forge. Its configuration and delivery log are stored in Atlassian's Forge hosted storage for your site. It sends space messages to the Google Chat webhooks you add, and personal notifications through the Spacecast Chat app to people who connect it. EAN Solutions runs no databases for Spacecast; the only service we run is the sign-in step for personal notifications at auth.ean-solutions.com, which stores nothing. See the privacy policy for the details.

Getting started

  1. Install Spacecast from the Atlassian Marketplace.
  2. In Google Chat, open a space, then Apps & integrations > Webhooks, and create a webhook. Name it Spacecast so messages in the space are clearly labeled. You can also give it an avatar URL, such as https://ean-solutions.com/spacecast/logo.png.
  3. In Jira, go to Settings > Apps > Spacecast for Google Chat, add a rule, paste the webhook URL, and press Test.

Your Google Workspace admin must allow incoming webhooks for Google Chat. See the full documentation for every rule option and troubleshooting.

Personal notifications in Google Chat

Besides posting to spaces, Spacecast can send each person their own Jira updates as direct messages from the Spacecast app in Google Chat:

  • issues assigned to them
  • mentions of them in comments and descriptions
  • status changes and comments on issues they watch

Each person chooses which of these they want and can limit them to certain projects, issue types, and priorities. Spacecast never messages someone about their own changes, only sends messages about issues the person can view in Jira, and never shows the text of restricted or internal comments. A Jira site admin can turn personal notifications off for the whole site.

Connecting your Google account

In Jira, open Apps > Spacecast: My notifications and click Connect Google Chat. Google asks you to let Spacecast:

  • See your email address, so the page can show which Google account is linked.
  • Create conversations in Google Chat, used once to open (or find) your direct message with the Spacecast app.

That is the only time Spacecast uses your Google sign-in. It does not request offline access, and it never reads your spaces, messages, or contacts. After connecting, the Spacecast app sends your notifications into that direct message on its own. Spacecast keeps only your Google user ID, email address, and the direct message's ID, linked to your Jira account.

To stop, click Disconnect on the same page; this deletes the link. You can also remove Spacecast under Third-party apps and services in your Google account. Personal notifications are rolling out to Jira sites as an app update that a site admin approves. See the privacy policy for how Google user data is handled.
