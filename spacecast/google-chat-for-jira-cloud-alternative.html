Moving off Google Chat for Jira Cloud

Google's "Google Chat for Jira Cloud" app is built on Atlassian Connect, and its last release was in November 2021. Atlassian ends support for Connect on 31 January 2027. This page explains what that means if your team relies on the app, and how to move your Jira notifications to Spacecast.

What is changing

Atlassian is replacing Connect, its older app framework, with Forge. According to Atlassian's developer documentation, Connect is no longer a supported platform from 31 January 2027. Three points matter for admins:

  • Installed Connect apps are not removed on that date. Atlassian has said customers will not lose access to them.
  • After that date nothing is guaranteed. Atlassian will fix only critical security problems in Connect, and says Connect features can be deprecated with little notice.
  • A Connect app can no longer be updated as a Connect app. To keep going, its vendor has to ship a Forge version.

The Marketplace listing for Google Chat for Jira Cloud shows version 1.1.0-AC, released 11 November 2021, as a Connect app. We have not seen an announcement from Google about a Forge version. If one is published, staying put may be the simplest choice. If you would rather not wait to find out, the move is small.

How to check what you have installed

  1. In Jira, go to Settings > Apps > Manage apps.
  2. Find Google Chat for Jira Cloud in the list of user-installed apps.
  3. In Google Chat, type /jira_settings in each space that gets Jira messages and note the projects and events it is subscribed to. That list is what you will recreate.

What Spacecast does not do. Spacecast sends notifications from Jira to Google Chat. It does not create or edit issues from Chat, and it does not respond to slash commands. If your team depends on those, Spacecast is not a full replacement.

Move in about ten minutes

  1. Install Spacecast from the Atlassian Marketplace. It is free for Jira sites with up to 10 users.
  2. In the Google Chat space, open Apps & integrations > Webhooks and add a webhook named Spacecast. Copy its URL.
  3. In Jira, go to Settings > Apps > Spacecast for Google Chat, add a rule, paste the webhook URL, choose the projects and events you noted earlier, and press Test.
  4. Let both apps post for a day. When the Spacecast messages look right, remove the old subscription with /jira_settings in that space.
  5. Repeat for each space. When none are left, uninstall Google Chat for Jira Cloud under Manage apps.

The documentation covers every rule option, digests, and troubleshooting.

Questions

Will Google Chat for Jira Cloud stop working on 31 January 2027?

Not necessarily on that day. Atlassian says installed Connect apps are not removed, but that Connect is unsupported from then on and its features can be deprecated with little notice. An app that has not moved to Forge may stop working properly at some point afterwards.

Can I import my existing settings?

No. The subscriptions are stored by Google's app and there is no export, so you recreate them one rule per space. Spacecast has templates for the common setups.

Can I run both at once?

Yes. They are independent, and running both for a day or two is the safest way to switch.

Does my Google Workspace admin need to do anything?

Incoming webhooks must be allowed for Google Chat, which is the default. Personal direct messages use the Spacecast Chat app from the Google Workspace Marketplace; if your admin only allows approved Chat apps, ask them to allow it.

Is Spacecast the only option?

No. For one or two simple alerts, a Jira Automation rule with a Send web request action and a Chat webhook works without any app. Several other Forge apps on the Atlassian Marketplace also send Jira updates to Google Chat. Spacecast is a fit when you want per-space rules with filters, one thread per issue, digests, and personal direct messages.
