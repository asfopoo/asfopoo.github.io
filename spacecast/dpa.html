Data Processing Addendum

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Spacecast Terms of Use (the "Terms") between EAN Solutions LLC ("Processor," "we," "us") and the organization that installs or uses Spacecast for Google Chat (the "Customer"). It applies automatically whenever we process Customer Personal Data on the Customer's behalf through Spacecast. No signature is needed. If your organization wants a countersigned copy, email support@ean-solutions.com.

1. Definitions

"Data Protection Laws" means all laws that apply to the processing of Customer Personal Data under the Terms, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("GDPR"), the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended ("CCPA"). "Customer Personal Data" means personal data in Jira content or app configuration that Spacecast processes for the Customer. "Controller," "processor," "data subject," "personal data breach," and "processing" have the meanings given in the GDPR. "Sub-processor" means a third party we engage to process Customer Personal Data.

2. Roles and instructions

The Customer is the controller (or a processor acting for its own controllers) and we are a processor. We will process Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions. The Terms, this DPA, and the Customer's configuration of Spacecast (its rules, filters, and chosen Google Chat webhooks) are the Customer's complete instructions. We will tell the Customer if we believe an instruction breaks Data Protection Laws. The Customer is responsible for the lawfulness of its instructions and for having a legal basis and any notices needed to send Jira content to Google Chat.

We will ensure that anyone we authorize to process Customer Personal Data is bound by confidentiality obligations.

5. Security

We will maintain the technical and organizational measures in Annex 1, which are designed to protect Customer Personal Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure, or access. We may update these measures as long as overall protection is not reduced.

6. Sub-processors

The Customer authorizes us to use the sub-processors listed in Annex 2. We will give at least 30 days' notice of a new sub-processor by updating this page and emailing the Customer's Marketplace license contacts. Google LLC is not our sub-processor. Google receives notifications only because the Customer directs Spacecast to send them to Google Chat webhooks the Customer controls, and Google processes them under the Customer's own Google Workspace agreement.

7. Data subject requests

Customer Personal Data lives in the Customer's Jira site and Forge storage, which the Customer administers. Taking into account the nature of the processing, we will provide reasonable help to the Customer in responding to data subject requests. If we receive a request directly, we will refer the data subject to the Customer.

8. Personal data breaches

We will notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data. We will provide the information reasonably available to us that the Customer needs to meet its own obligations, and take reasonable steps to contain and remedy the breach.

9. Deletion

Delivery log entries are deleted automatically after 7 days. Deleting a rule deletes its settings and webhook URL. When Spacecast is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the app's stored data for that site under its Forge data retention rules. We keep no other copy of Customer Personal Data, except as the law requires.

10. Assistance and audits

We will give the Customer reasonable help with data protection impact assessments and prior consultations, taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to us. We will make available the information reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, including written answers to security questionnaires. If Data Protection Laws or a regulator require more, the Customer may audit our compliance once a year with at least 30 days' notice, at its own cost, during business hours, and under confidentiality terms. Because Spacecast runs on Atlassian's Forge platform, audits of the hosting environment are covered by Atlassian's own certifications and reports.

Annex 1: Technical and organizational measures

  • Hosting. Spacecast runs entirely on Atlassian Forge. We operate no servers, databases, or storage of our own for Spacecast.
  • Encryption. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by Atlassian. Webhook URLs are kept in Forge encrypted secret storage and are never returned to the browser.
  • Network egress. The app manifest limits outbound requests to chat.googleapis.com.
  • Least privilege. The app requests only the scopes read:jira-work, read:jira-user, and storage:app.
  • Access control. Only Jira site admins can manage Spacecast. Project admins can manage rules for their own project only, and site admins can turn that off.
  • Data minimization. Jira event content is processed in memory and not stored. Comment text sent to Chat is capped at 600 characters. App logs contain the event type and issue key, not issue content.
  • Retention. Delivery log entries expire after 7 days. Settings are deleted with the rule or on uninstall.
  • Vendor access. We cannot browse the Customer's Jira site or app storage. Our developer accounts are protected with multi-factor authentication.
  • Incident response. We monitor app errors and respond to security reports sent to support@ean-solutions.com, with breach notice as described in section 8.
