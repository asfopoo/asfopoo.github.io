Data Processing Addendum

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Spacecast Terms of Use (the "Terms") between EAN Solutions LLC ("Processor," "we," "us") and the organization that installs or uses Spacecast for Google Chat (the "Customer"). It applies automatically whenever we process Customer Personal Data on the Customer's behalf through Spacecast. No signature is needed. If your organization wants a countersigned copy, email support@ean-solutions.com.

1. Definitions

"Data Protection Laws" means all laws that apply to the processing of Customer Personal Data under the Terms, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("GDPR"), the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended ("CCPA"). "Customer Personal Data" means personal data in Jira content or app configuration that Spacecast processes for the Customer. "Controller," "processor," "data subject," "personal data breach," and "processing" have the meanings given in the GDPR. "Sub-processor" means a third party we engage to process Customer Personal Data.

2. Roles and instructions

The Customer is the controller (or a processor acting for its own controllers) and we are a processor. We will process Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions. The Terms, this DPA, and the Customer's configuration of Spacecast (its rules, filters, chosen Google Chat webhooks, and whether personal notifications are allowed), together with the personal notification settings of the Customer's users, are the Customer's complete instructions. We will tell the Customer if we believe an instruction breaks Data Protection Laws. The Customer is responsible for the lawfulness of its instructions and for having a legal basis and any notices needed to send Jira content to Google Chat.

6. Sub-processors

The Customer authorizes us to use the sub-processors listed in Annex 2. We will give at least 30 days' notice of a new sub-processor by updating this page and emailing the Customer's Marketplace license contacts. The Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. If we cannot address the objection, the Customer may uninstall Spacecast, and any prepaid fees for the remaining term will be handled under Atlassian's Marketplace refund policies. For space notifications sent through Google Chat webhooks, Google LLC is not our sub-processor: Google receives those notifications only because the Customer directs Spacecast to send them to webhooks the Customer controls, and processes them under the Customer's own Google Workspace agreement. For personal notifications, which Spacecast sends through the Google Chat API as the Spacecast Chat app using a Google Cloud project we own, and for the Google sign-in used to connect them, Google LLC is our sub-processor as listed in Annex 2.

7. Data subject requests

Customer Personal Data lives in the Customer's Jira site and Forge storage, which the Customer administers. Taking into account the nature of the processing, we will provide reasonable help to the Customer in responding to data subject requests. If we receive a request directly, we will refer the data subject to the Customer.

8. Personal data breaches

We will notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data. We will provide the information reasonably available to us that the Customer needs to meet its own obligations, and take reasonable steps to contain and remedy the breach.

9. Deletion

Delivery log entries are deleted automatically after 7 days. Digest queue entries are deleted when the digest is posted, and after 8 days at most. Deleting a rule deletes its settings, webhook URL, and digest queue. When a user clicks Disconnect, or a site admin removes their link, their personal notification link and preferences are deleted. When Spacecast is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the app's stored data for that site under its Forge data retention rules. We keep no other copy of Customer Personal Data, except as the law requires.

10. Assistance and audits

We will give the Customer reasonable help with data protection impact assessments and prior consultations, taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to us. We will make available the information reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, including written answers to security questionnaires. If Data Protection Laws or a regulator require more, the Customer may audit our compliance once a year with at least 30 days' notice, at its own cost, during business hours, and under confidentiality terms. Because Spacecast runs on Atlassian's Forge platform, audits of the hosting environment are covered by Atlassian's own certifications and reports.

Annex 1: Technical and organizational measures

  • Hosting. Spacecast runs on Atlassian Forge. We operate no databases or storage of our own for Spacecast. The only component we operate is a stateless sign-in service at auth.ean-solutions.com (hosted on Vercel) used when a user connects personal notifications; it stores no Customer Personal Data, its own logs record only success or failure, and Vercel's standard request logs (IP address and time) are kept briefly under Vercel's retention.
  • Encryption. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by Atlassian. Webhook URLs are kept in Forge encrypted secret storage and are never returned to the browser. The Spacecast Chat app's Google service account key is kept in encrypted Forge environment variables. Users' Google sign-in tokens are used once by the sign-in service and revoked immediately; they are not stored anywhere. Results passed from the sign-in service to the app are signed with a shared secret and accepted only once, within minutes of the request.
  • Network egress. The app manifest limits outbound requests to chat.googleapis.com and oauth2.googleapis.com.
  • Least privilege. The app requests only the Jira scopes read:jira-work, read:jira-user, and storage:app. For personal notifications it requests only the Google scopes openid, email, and chat.spaces.create from users, without offline access, and chat.bot for the app itself. It does not read Chat messages.
  • Access control. Only Jira site admins can manage Spacecast. Project admins can manage rules for their own project only, and site admins can turn that off. Each user manages only their own personal notifications, and site admins can turn personal notifications off for everyone.
  • Data minimization. Jira event content is processed in memory and not stored, except that rules set to an hourly or daily digest queue the issue key, summary, type, status, event type, status change, and the display names of the actor and new assignee until the digest is posted (no comment or description text). Comment text sent to Chat is capped at 600 characters and description previews at 300. Personal direct messages go only to users who can view the issue, never about their own changes, and hide the text of comments with restricted visibility. App logs contain the event type, issue key, and delivery counts, not issue content or recipients.
  • Retention. Delivery log entries expire after 7 days; digest queue entries after 8 days at most. Settings are deleted with the rule or on uninstall.
  • Vendor access. We cannot browse the Customer's Jira site or app storage. Our developer accounts are protected with multi-factor authentication.
  • Incident response. We monitor app errors and respond to security reports sent to support@ean-solutions.com, with breach notice as described in section 8.
